## 1. Method: what changed against the first report

**Rule sets.** Coverage (L17-L22), start/sit (L13-L16), the D-078 bootstrap (500 week-clustered paired draws, seed 0, 95% percentile intervals), the metric seed and the populations are unchanged in every candidate. Only the accuracy lines differ:

- `R0` (22 lines): the 22 lines as written: per position and baseline the chain beats on MAE, pinball q10, q50 and q90; plus L13-L22.
- `R1` (28 lines): per position and baseline not worse on all four metrics (12); QB-TE pooled beats on MAE and pinball q50 (6); plus L13-L22.
- `R2` (31 lines): per position and baseline beats on MAE and not worse on the three pinball losses (12); QB-TE pooled beats on pinball q10, q50, q90 (9); plus L13-L22.
- `R3` (34 lines): R1 with QB-TE pooled beats on MAE, pinball q10, q50 and q90 (12); plus L13-L22.

**Not worse** on a metric means the 95% interval of chain minus comparator is not entirely above 0 (the mirror of **beats**, entirely below 0); a missing interval fails either way, as in `evaluate_lines`. A per-position "not worse on all four metrics" line fails when any one metric's interval lies entirely above 0.

**Pooled QB-TE lines.** The rows of QB, RB, WR and TE are pooled into one population; MAE and the pinball losses are then one ratio of sums over all those rows, bootstrapped over the same week clusters with the same draws (every week has rows at each of the four positions, so the cluster universe is the window's weeks). This is what `metric_bundle` gives on the pooled frame; the fast path reproduces it (section 2).

**No-edge null (N0).** The brief's shifted-C construction with k = 0, made exact per week number: for each week number w, comparator and metric, every per-row chain-minus-comparator loss difference is moved by its 2022-2024 mean at week w (the ratio of sums over the three slates of that week number), so its expectation is 0 at every week number, for the per-position and the pooled lines alike, while the week-to-week noise is kept. Start/sit differences against the consensus are centred the same way. Coverage is scenario A's (well calibrated). The variance-corrected null (N0_vc) applies the sqrt(3/2) stretch of A_vc first; the stretch keeps every week-number mean, so the centring is unchanged. With the mean at zero the stretch is a pure scale of every accuracy and start/sit difference and of its bootstrap draws, and the sign rule is scale-invariant, so N0_vc and N0 give the same accuracy and start/sit verdicts; they differ only through coverage (L17-L22), as the tables show.

**Windows.** (i) weeks 10-18 and (ii) weeks 11-18 with the 2026 game counts, as before; (ext18) 2026 weeks 10-18 plus 2027 weeks 1-9 drawn the same way, full slates for weeks 1-9 (the 2027 schedule is unknown); (one miss) weeks 10-18 with one week, drawn uniformly, excluded from scoring (D-106: eight weeks, no extension). Windows (i) and (ii) use the random streams of the first report, so `R0` here is that report's first 5,000 replicates.

**Selection (D-105).** A candidate qualifies if its no-edge pass rate (N0_vc, weeks 10-18) is at most 10% and its good-model pass rate (A_vc, weeks 10-18) is at least 70%. Among qualifying candidates the lowest false-approval rate wins; a candidate whose rate is within 2 standard errors of the difference from that minimum counts as close, and among close candidates the one with the fewest lines wins.

## 8. Assumptions, in addition to the first report's

1. **The null is a construction.** A no-edge model is represented by the validated rows with each accuracy and start/sit difference centred on 0 per week number; its week-to-week noise is the chain's and the baselines' real noise. The centring is exact for full slates; with games dropped it holds to within the thinning's rounding (the three seasons of a week number are thinned by slightly different shares).
2. **Coverage under the null is scenario A's.** The false-approval rate therefore counts a no-edge model that is well calibrated; a no-edge model with poor coverage would fail L17-L22 more often.
3. **Variance correction.** A_vc and N0_vc stretch each simulated week's deviation from its week-number mean by sqrt(3/2), the expected shortfall of a pick among three slates; the correction is approximate and applied to every statistic alike.
4. **The 18-week window** uses full 2022-2024 slates for weeks 1-9 because the 2027 schedule is not known; weeks 1-9 are drawn independently of weeks 10-18 (no season-level carry-over).
5. **One miss** is drawn uniformly over the nine weeks; a real miss is more likely in some weeks than others (holiday weeks), which is not modelled.
6. **Pooled lines are new code paths.** The holdout report (`report_holdout.py`) does not compute a pooled QB-TE group; adopting R1-R3 requires adding that group to the real report. The simulation evaluates it with `metric_bundle` on the pooled frame, which is the definition the report would use.
7. **Selection operationalisation.** "Close" in D-105 item 2 is read as within 2 standard errors of the difference between two candidates' false-approval rates, fixed here before the numbers were seen.
