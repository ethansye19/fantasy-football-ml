## Defects found and fixed during review

- The Sleeper rate-limiter test depended on wall-clock time and failed in CI;
  QA made it deterministic.
- Fine field-goal distance buckets were merged away in fact_player_game
  although league scoring distinguishes six bands; restored, with sums
  verified against the raw stats (2025: 4, 213, 292, 276, 179, 12 = 976).

## Open items carried into Phase 3 (not gate blockers)

- `started` flag (depth-chart rank 1 at cutoff) is derived by the feature
  builder from fact_depth_chart, not stored in fact_player_game.
- `offense_snaps` is null where Pro Football Reference has no row.
- Retention policy "one snapshot per completed week forever" is not yet
  automated (store supports prune by count).
- The restricted rankings archive is private evaluation only; its data must
  never be committed, served, or shown in a public report (D-007).
