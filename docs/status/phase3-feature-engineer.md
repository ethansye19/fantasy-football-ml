Done:
- `ffml/features/spec.py`: `FEATURE_VERSION = "0.1.0"`, `PARAMS` (EW alpha,
  the blend weights, as-of offset, the `shrink_k` pseudo-counts: targets 20,
  carries 30, attempts 60, TD rates 100, plus documented keys for receptions,
  dropbacks, FG/PAT, sparse shares, defense and DST), the naming helper and
  the `Stat` spec that drives both computation and manifest.
- `ffml/features/windows.py`: per-entity game states (rolling sums, EW with
  un-normalised sums for shrinkage, career and season cumulative sums, all as
  Polars window expressions); the strict as-of join on `cutoff_ts`; PS
  totals; position or league priors from strictly earlier seasons; the
  finaliser for the STD blend, shrinkage, `_n` columns and the team-change
  mask. Float sums are ordered cumulative sums, so builds are bit-reproducible.
- `ffml/features/data.py`: `FeatureContext`, loaded from the processed store
  (LATEST, column-pruned, depth chart filtered to skill slots) or from a
  fixture directory, with `truncate()` and `with_tables()` for the tests. The
  week calendar gives `cutoff_ts` (first kickoff of the week) and
  `asof_ts = cutoff_ts - 1h`.
- `ffml/features/targets.py`: historical targets for every fact row with the
  `y_` outcomes of FEATURES.md section I (60 player, 17 team, 9 DST), and
  `future_targets(season, week)`. The latter builds rows from dim_game games
  not yet final and the latest fact_roster_week (ACT or INA, QB/RB/WR/TE/K),
  with `asof_ts = now` and null targets.
- `ffml/features/context.py`: game context (group A); depth chart as of
  `asof_ts` with ranks harmonised between the legacy and 2025+ formats; QB1;
  lagged injury features; the same-week `avail__` columns.
- `ffml/features/team.py`: groups B and C with the "offense on the field /
  defense on the field" convention, and `team__qb_change_flag`.
- `ffml/features/player.py`: groups D, E and F and the K inputs. Shares reset

Processed-table findings (not patched; for the Data Engineer):
- fact_player_game has roster-only rows (`played = false`) for unfinished
  games: 33 for `2026_08_CHI_SEA` (scheduled for 2026-11-03) and 35 for
  `2026_08_PHI_WAS` (in progress at the 02:39Z build). Those zeros are not
  outcomes. The feature layer now nulls every `y_` for rows whose dim_game
  status is not final (68 rows in 2026); the builder should add roster rows
  only for final games.
- fact_player_game has field goals made per distance band but attempts and
  misses only in total, so FEATURES.md H "fg_pct by distance" cannot be built.
  Adding `fg_att_<band>` or `fg_missed_<band>` would fix this, and league
  scoring with miss penalties by distance may need it too.
- `fact_injury_report.snapshot_ts` is the ingest time for every row
  (2026-11-02), so it cannot serve as an information time. Audits use
  `report_modified_at` (2016-2024: all 49,384 timestamped rows that have a
  following week fall before that week's cutoff) or the report week's last
  kickoff (2025 onward).
- 47 roster-only fact_player_game rows (2016: 42, 2022: 1, 2024: 4) have a
  player_id that is not in dim_player.
- `dim_game.surface` has values with a trailing space (`"grass "`, 93 games)
  and empty strings (98 games); the feature layer strips them.
- fact_roster_week 2026 week 8 has 13 INA rows against 180-202 in weeks 1-7,
  probably captured before inactives were set. future_targets includes ACT
  and INA, so nothing is lost, but availability rules should not read INA
  from the newest week.

Decisions proposed:
- D-049 Legacy depth-chart timing. With the D-037 synthetic stamp (first
  kickoff minus 48 h) and D-046's "latest snapshot at or before asof_ts", week
  w's legacy chart is used for week w rows in 2016-2024. FEATURES.md ground
  rule 1 asked for week w-1 where no real timestamp exists. Proposal: accept
  the D-046 behaviour (nflverse weekly charts are pre-game releases), amend
  ground rule 1, and have the evaluator run an ablation without depth
  features on 2022-2024.
- D-050 Window semantics as built:
  - windows are ratios of sums;
  - `std` is the blended value (raw season-to-date is not published;
    `_n` gives sample sizes);
  - shares reset L1-STD on team change while PS and CAR stay player level;
  - shrinkage priors are position (or league) totals of all earlier
    seasons, so 2016 rows have null shrunk columns;
  - `team__` and `def__` mean offense and defense on the field (in
    dst_week, `team__` is the opponent's offense).
- D-051 Injury information time for audits is `report_modified_at`, or the
  report week's last kickoff where nflverse publishes none; never
  `fact_injury_report.snapshot_ts`.
- D-052 Targets are null for any row whose game is not final in dim_game,
  and fact_player_game should add roster rows only for final games.
