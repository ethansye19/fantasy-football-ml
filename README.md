# Gridiron Projections (working title)

An original, ML-powered fantasy football analytics app: weekly projections with
floor / median / ceiling and boom-bust probabilities, start/sit comparisons with
explained drivers, waiver-wire rankings for a specific roster, a trade analyzer,
and rest-of-season rankings. League setup via Sleeper import or manual settings,
with custom scoring and roster rules applied at request time (no retraining).

**Status:** no approved model. The app shows "no approved model" and serves
no projections until a model passes its pre-registered test, expected about
2027-11-12. See [Current status](#current-status) below,
[docs/PLAN.md](docs/PLAN.md) for phases and gates, and
[docs/DECISIONS.md](docs/DECISIONS.md) for the decision log.

  - In production every projection route answers `no_approved_model`. Projections, start/sit, waivers, trades and rest-of-season rankings are off. Labeled private tests are the exception (D-099, D-100, D-101).
- **The chain_v2 live test.**
  - chain_v2 adds 2025 to training and updates its calibration weekly (D-098). It is under a pre-registered live test: the unchanged 22 lines over 2026 weeks 10-18 and 2027 weeks 1-9, with one verdict expected about 2027-11-12 ([docs/status/livetest-preregistration-2026.md](docs/status/livetest-preregistration-2026.md); D-110, D-111, D-119).

  - On 2025 the chain beat the 4-game rolling average, the prior-season average and the 8-game rolling median on MAE and pinball loss at QB, RB, WR and TE (lines L01-L12).

- **Against a public expert consensus.** The chain did not beat it on MAE at any position on 2025, and it was worse at QB and RB. Start/sit was not worse at any position (D-097).
- **Boom/bust.** QB, K and DST boom/bust probabilities are low confidence and are hidden or marked in the app (D-089, D-104).
- **Open before Gate 6.**
  - authentication (QA-F14);
  - the deployment guide;
  - an end-to-end CI job;
  - a recorded fresh install.
- **Docker.** Docker is verified in CI only (D-004).

## Data attribution (summary)

Play-by-play, player and team statistics, schedules, rosters, injuries, depth
charts, Next Gen Stats, and Pro Football Reference snap counts are provided by
the [nflverse](https://nflverse.nflverse.com/) project under CC-BY-4.0.
FTN charting data, if used, is CC-BY-SA-4.0 and must be attributed to
"ftndata.com via nflverse". League data comes from the public Sleeper API
(non-commercial use). A full attribution page ships with the app.

Nothing in this project places bets or presents any pick as guaranteed.
